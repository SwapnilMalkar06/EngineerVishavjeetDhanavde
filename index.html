METHODOLOGY — 04

How a Unique Construction project moves from sketch to handover.

A predictable, completely transparent 5-stage engineering delivery workflow — refined over 120+ successful structures — so you always know what is happening on your site.

01

Site Survey & Consultation

Geotechnical soil assessments, topography surveys, boundary checking, and detailed client brief sessions.

02

Design & Estimation

Architectural layout modeling, structural frame loading estimations, and detailed bills of quantities (BOQ).

03

Permits & Approvals

End-to-end statutory liaison with town municipal corporations, environmental boards, and PWD desks.

04

Execution & Supervision

Site grading, reinforcement framing, concrete pours, QA/QC reports, and weekly structured client briefs.

05

Snagging & Handover

Structural inspections, final cleaning, warranty documentation handovers, and defect liability support.
